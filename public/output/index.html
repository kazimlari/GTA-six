So, what is it?

CouchVolt lets you stream a real PS5 Pro, PS5 or Xbox Series X that lives in a datacenter straight into Chrome. That means you can play GTA VI and other games on a laptop, Mac or Chromebook without owning a console.

The site says it uses real console hardware, not emulation, and that it also works in TV browsers.

What you’ll need

  • A fiber or stable 50 Mbps+ internet connection
  • An Ethernet cable, or 5 GHz Wi-Fi close to your router
  • Google Chrome or another Chromium browser
  • An Xbox or PlayStation controller (USB or Bluetooth)
  • Your own PlayStation or Xbox account. The site says you sign in with your own account and that games are not included, so it’s worth checking you have access to the game.

Let’s get you playing

  1. Create your free account

    Head to couchvolt.com and use the sign-up form. The site says it emails you a code, so there’s no password to remember.

  2. Check your connection

    Try the connection test on the homepage. It takes about two seconds and shows your round-trip time to the nearest server. Lower is better!

  3. Choose a console

    The Consoles section shows live machines, such as Xbox Series X or PS5 Pro, with their location and status (Available, In use, or Playing). Pick one that’s available.

  4. Connect your controller

    Plug in or pair your Xbox or PlayStation controller by USB or Bluetooth before you launch.

  5. Sign in with your own account

    Per the site, you log in with your own PlayStation or Xbox account to play.

  6. Launch GTA VI and enjoy

    Select Grand Theft Auto VI from the games list and jump in. Up to 4K at 120 FPS is advertised on a good connection.

  7. Console busy? No problem

    If everything is in use, choose Get notified and the site will let you know when one opens up.

The homepage says the Cloud Gaming Pass is ad-supported with no subscription and no card needed. For Cloud Console Pass details, it’s best to check the site directly.

Tips for a smooth session

  • Use a wired connection if you can. It really helps.
  • Pause other downloads or streams on your network while you play.
  • If you’re on Wi-Fi, sit close to the router and use the 5 GHz band.
  • Pick a console in a region near you for lower latency.
